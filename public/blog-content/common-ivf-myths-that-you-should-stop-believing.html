Common IVF Myths and Misconceptions: What Patients Must Know?

Some myths and misconceptions about IVF are quite common, and patients must know the truth before they opt for opt for the treatment.

IVF Always Leads to a Successful Pregnancy

That is not always the case. Although the process increases the chances of pregnancy, there is no absolute guarantee of its success. Various important factors such as age, quality of sperm, quality of eggs and overall health tend to play a big role.

IVF Always Leads to Multiple Pregnancies

Many people think that IVF conducted at the best IVF center in Kolkata leads to the birth of twins, triplets, etc. Multiple pregnancies occur quite often with IVF due to multiple embryo transfers, but that is not always the case. Generally, fertility specialists try to help women achieve just one pregnancy. The chances of multiple births have gone down today due to advances in techniques and technologies.

IVF is Only for Older Women

For couples of any age who are unable to conceive normally, IVF can be a viable option. Due to the quality of their sperm and eggs, chances of success are more for younger couples who have age and health working in their favor.

IVF is too Costly

IVF can be expensive, but not necessarily more when compared to other surgical methods used to fix infertility issues. Based on the particular clinic that you opt for and the number of cycles that you need, the IVF treatment price can vary.

IVF Should be Regarded as a Last Resort

Based on individual circumstances, IVF may be recommended earlier in the infertility journey. It is among the most important fertility treatments available today.

IVF is Risky or Unnatural

A natural process, IVF helps with the process of fertilization. Babies delivered through IVF are just like those born out of natural conception.

IVF Involves Many Painful Procedures

Typically, the discomfort is temporary or mild, and IVF procedures are tolerated well enough.
