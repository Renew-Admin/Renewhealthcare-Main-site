Types of treatment :

The type of treatment required can also affect the cost. Basic IVF treatment, which involves the use of medication to stimulate ovulation and the retrieval of eggs, is typically less expensive than more advanced treatments such as ICSI (intracytoplasmic sperm injection) or PICSI (polar body intracytoplasmic sperm injection), which are used to treat more severe cases of infertility. In addition to these factors, other costs such as the cost of any necessary tests or ultrasounds, the cost of medications, and the cost of any additional procedures, such as egg or sperm donation, can also affect the final price of IVF in Kolkata.
