What Is the HSG Test?

The HSG full form is hysterosalpingography, and the imaging study itself is called a hysterosalpingogram. The HSG test means a diagnostic X-ray procedure that is used to examine the uterus and fallopian tubes. During the Hysterosalpingogram (HSG), a special contrast dye is inserted into the uterus through the cervix. X-ray images are then taken to observe how the dye flows through the uterus and fallopian tubes. This HSG test process helps doctors clearly visualize the reproductive organs. If the dye moves freely through the tubes and spills into the pelvic cavity, the tubes are considered open. But if the dye stops at any point, it can indicate a blocked fallopian tube, which can affect fertility. That is why the HSG fallopian tube test is also known as a fallopian tube blockage test. It also helps explain how HSG test helps detect blocked fallopian tubes, which is one of the most important aspects of infertility diagnosis.

Why Is an HSG Test Done?

Doctors might recommend the HSG test to investigate infertility. Understanding why HSG test is done helps patients feel more informed and comfortable during the diagnostic process. This test can be instrumental in identifying structural or functional issues in the reproductive system that might be preventing pregnancy. In fact, what is an HSG test and why is it done for infertility is one of the most common questions asked during fertility consultations.
  1. Detect blocked fallopian tubes
One of the most common causes of infertility is fallopian tube blockage. The HSG blocked fallopian tube test shows whether the tubes are open and functioning normally. This explains how HSG test helps detect blocked fallopian tubes.
  1. Evaluating the shape of the uterus
The HSG test helps detect abnormalities in the uterine cavity that may affect implantation.
  1. Investigate previous pregnancy complications
Women who have experienced miscarriage or ectopic pregnancy can undergo hysterosalpingography to evaluate reproductive anatomy.
  1. To confirm tubal ligation
In some cases, an HSG test may be done after tubal ligation to confirm that the tubes are fully blocked.

HSG Test Procedure: Step-by-Step

The HSG test is performed in the radiology department and only takes a couple of minutes. Doctors generally schedule the HSG procedure 7 to 8 days after the first day of the menstrual cycle. This timing ensures that the test is done before ovulation. Understanding the HSG test procedure step by step for infertility diagnosis can help reduce anxiety before the test. Before the test, it's very important that the patients should inform the doctor if they have:
  • Have allergies to iodine or contrast dye
  • Have a pelvic infection or sexually transmitted infection
  • Have heavy vaginal bleeding
  • Might be pregnant

What Conditions Can an HSG Test Detect?

The fallopian tube test can reveal certain reproductive conditions that might be affecting fertility. This further explains how HSG test helps detect blocked fallopian tubes and other structural problems. Some conditions related to fallopian tubes include fallopian tube blockage, damage from sexually transmitted infections, tubal spasms, scarring from previous infections, or effects of prior ectopic pregnancy. FAQs

Is the HSG Test Painful?

The HSG test may cause mild cramping during the dye injection. Most women describe this discomfort as temporary and manageable. Pain usually subsides within a few minutes to a few hours.

When is an HSG test recommended for infertility evaluation?

Doctors can recommend the HSG fallopian tube test when couples experience difficulty conceiving. This test is used to check for fallopian tube blockage or uterine abnormalities.

Can I get pregnant after an HSG test?

Yes, many doctors say that it is safe to try for pregnancy shortly after the HSG test. In some cases, the dye used in hysterosalpingography can help clear minor blockages, which can help improve fertility temporarily.

How long does the HSG procedure take?

The HST test procedure is very quick and takes about 5 minutes to complete. If you include preparation time, you may spend around 15 to 20 minutes on the whole thing.

What are the benefits of an HSG test?

The benefits of HSG test for women are that it can help detect blockages in the fallopian tubes, identify uterine abnormalities, and provide clear imaging for accurate diagnosis. The procedure can also help in temporarily improving fertility by flushing minor blockages.

Are there any risks or side effects of an HSG test?

The HSG test is generally safe, but like any other medical procedure, there can be mild side effects. Some women may experience temporary cramping, light spotting, or slight discomfort after the HSG test process.
