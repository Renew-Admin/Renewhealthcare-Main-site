What Is Sperm?

Sperm are male reproductive cells made in the testes. They are part of semen, along with other fluids from the prostate and seminal vesicles. A full sperm production cycle takes around 2-3 months. At the same time, your body keeps making new sperm continuously to replenish what’s used.

When Is Sperm Released and How Does It Happen?
