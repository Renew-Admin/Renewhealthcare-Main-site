What is Embryo Glue?
Embryo glue is an addition to the process, which helps in fixing the issues of implantation. For an IVF to work successfully, the doctors create an environment similar to a woman’s uterus, for the successful fertilization of the Embryo. One important acid present in a woman’s uterus is Hyaluronic acid. To create an efficient environment, doctors add hyaluronic acid to the embryo transfer media. Hyaluronic acid is rich in amino acids, carbohydrates, and proteins, which are crucial for the proper development of the embryo. It is used in the artificial environment as it helps to stick the embryo to the womb. Thereby. diminishing the activities of the embryo in the womb.
Benefits of Embryo Glue
The Embryo Glue helps in keeping the Embryo intact within the womb. Therefore, doctors mainly recommend this to patients who have experienced a failed IVF treatment. According to various researches, couples using Embryo Glue have a 7% increase in their success rate. Doctors also prescribe this to patients who are trying the IVF treatment for the first time. Couples should always consult their doctors regarding the use of Embryo Glue. However, if the previous IVF treatment fails, the doctor is likely to prescribe Embryo Glue for the next attempt. Embryo Glue is used in the last stage of embryo transfer, used for IVF procedures. However, it does not possess any threats.
