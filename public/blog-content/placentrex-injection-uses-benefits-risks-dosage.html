What is Placentrex Injection?

The placentrex injection is a placenta extract injection which is derived from human placenta. It contains bioactive compounds such as peptides, amino acids, enzymes, vitamins, growth factors and cytokines. These components work together to:
  • Stimulate tissue repair
  • Improve blood circulation
  • Reduce inflammation
  • Support immune function

Placentrex Injection Side Effects and Potential Risks You Should Know

Though generally well tolerated, placentrex injection side effects can include:
  • Pain at injection site
  • Redness or swelling
  • Mild irritation

Rare but Serious Risks

  • Allergic reactions
  • Breathing difficulty
  • Skin rash or swelling
If you experience severe symptoms, it is very important to seek immediate medical attention

Who Should Avoid Using Placentrex Injection

Inj Placentrex injection may not be suitable for everyone. Caution is advised for:
  • Patients with placenta extract allergies
  • Individuals with active infections
  • Those with autoimmune disorders
  • Patients with blood clotting disorders
  • Certain cancer conditions
