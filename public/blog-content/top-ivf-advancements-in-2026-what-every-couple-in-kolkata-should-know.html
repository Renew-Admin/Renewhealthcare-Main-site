Platelet-Rich Plasma (PRP) Therapy

This is one of the biggest advancements in IVF treatment especially in women with poor ovarian reserve or thin endometrial lining. PRP is derived from the blood of patients themselves. It contains growth factors and concentrated platelets. When these are introduced into the ovaries or uterus it can stimulate the cells and help with repair and regeneration. Earlier, women were told that they could not conceive due to thin endometrial lining. However, they are now getting a second shot at pregnancy through PRP. Intra ovarian PRP is essentially giving hope and success to women with poor ovarian reserve. You can find an IVF specialist in Kolkata now offering this therapy as a supplementary treatment for improving the potential of implantation and as well as ovarian function. If you have experienced repeated failures with your IVF treatment, then you can go for this therapy and renew your fertility strategy.

Lymphocyte Immunization Therapy (LIT)

Traditional IVF treatment focuses on embryos, sperm and eggs. However, LIT takes a different approach and addresses an important aspect, which is the immune response. It so happens that in some cases, the immune system of a woman may treat the embryo as a foreign object and start attacking it. When this happens, implantation fails and there can be recurrent miscarriages. In LIT, lymphocytes or white blood cells from the male partner are injected into the female partner to help her body develop embryo tolerance. In 2026, the LIT protocol has become more advanced. It is now used in a better way with more chances of success. In IVF in Kolkata clinics, you can increasingly find LIT being recommended for couples who face multiple pregnancy losses or unexplained fertility. It is an office procedure, requires no additional preparation and is well tolerated. Although you can find some physicians and researchers in the medical circle debating this type of treatment, it is becoming more and more popular in specialized fertility centers across Kolkata, due to better patient outcomes.

Immunological Assessment

Earlier, IVF treatment often failed because Doctors could not detect subtle immune disorders that affected fertility. However, with the advanced immunological assessments in 2026, doctors can now test women for:
  • Elevated Natural Killer (NK) cell activity
  • Antiphospholipid antibodies
  • HLA compatibility issues
  • Thyroid autoantibodies
  • Inflammatory cytokine imbalances
These tests are now a part of even affordable IVF treatment in Salt Lake. With these types of exams specialists in IVF can get a better insight into failure of embryo implantation and recurrent miscarriage in some women. Based on such assessments, they can develop custom treatment plans, which may include steroids, blood thinners, IV immunoglobulin (IVIG), or LIT.
