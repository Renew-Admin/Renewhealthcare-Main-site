Egg freezing (oocyte cryopreservation) is a special process that is used to extract the eggs of a woman and freeze it to be used at a later time. The eggs are generally needed in IVF (in vitro fertilization) at the best IVF center in Kolkata, to achieve pregnancy. When Should Consider Freezing Your Eggs - What is the Best Age?

After you are 18 years old, you can consider freezing your eggs. Here are the various age groups:
  • 18+: In India, 18 years is the minimum age for egg freezing. You should consult with a fertility specialist to determine the best egg freezing timing, based on what your individual situation is. A specialist can undoubtedly help you to make an informed decision.
  • From the late 20s to the early 30s: Generally, this age group is regarded as the optimal age to freeze eggs. Often, people of this age range are typically regarded as in the best health and the quality of eggs happens to be at its peak. This leads to higher success rates for egg freezers in the future.
  • After 35 years of age: It is possible to freeze eggs even after this age. However, there is a drop in success rates, as there is a natural decline in the quality of eggs with age.
Keep in mind that there is no change in the egg freezing process based on your sexual experience and whether or not you are a virgin. You do not even need to tell your IVF doctor in Kolkata about this when you express your interest to perform an egg freezing procedure.

Before You Go for the Egg Freezing Process: Top Things You Should Know

Before you begin the process, you must discuss your medical history with a fertility specialist. He is likely to assess your ovarian reserve, and determine what needs to be done. Before you go for the process at an IVF center in Kolkata, discuss these important, relevant factors with him:
  • Your lifestyle
  • Your age and success potential of the process for you
  • Cost of the procedure and whether it will be covered by your medical insurance
  • Side effects and potential risks
Once the two of you take into account all these factors, you can make an informed decision on whether or not to go ahead with the procedure.
