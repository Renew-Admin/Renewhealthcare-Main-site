Factors Affecting IVF Success Rates

There are 7 common factors influencing IVF outcomes that need to be considered when talking about the highest IVF success rates:

1. Age Of Female

Age is one of the most important factors while thinking about IVF success rate. As a biological process, fertility in females starts to decline after 35 years. Let’s understand IVF success rates by age:
Age Success Rate
Under 35 40% to 50%
35 to 37 30% to 40%
38 to 40 20% to 30%
41 to 42 10% to 15%
Above 42 Below 10%
With increasing age, the quality and quantity of egg decreases, thus affecting fertility, embryo development and implantation. IVF success rate in women is usually better in early 30s i.e., before 35 years. Read Our Detailed Guide On: How Age Affects Fertility

2. Egg Quality and Ovaries

Egg quality is an essential factor for creating a healthy embryo. To check the quality of eggs, fertility experts evaluate ovarian reserves with tests such as AFC (Antral Follicle Count) and AMH (Anti Mullerian Hormone). If the no’s for ovarian reserve are in a low side, chances are higher that low no of eggs are retrieved during IVF, thus impacting IVF success rate.

3. Sperm Quality

Success of IVF depends on both the couples fertility health. Male fertility factors such as sperm motility, count, morphology, DNA also influences embryo quality thus overall IVF treatment success rate. IVF experts at Renew Healthcare with hands-on experience on advanced fertility testing techniques help in identifying the male fertility issues and then suggest suitable treatment options.

4. Embryo Quality

Another important factor affecting IVF success rate in India is the quality of embryo. The better the quality of embryo, higher are the chances of successful implantation and healthy pregnancy ahead. In addition to the above, other things that can increase IVF success rates include:
  • Embryo selection: By choosing the best quality embryo for transfer, the chances of a successful pregnancy are increased.
  • Transferring fewer embryos: Transferring more embryos increases the risk of multiple pregnancies and therefore reduces the chances of having a healthy single baby.
  • Timing the transfer: transfer of embryos at the right time increases the chances of implantation.

5. Already Existing Health Conditions

Some underlying health conditions also affect the chances of IVF success and fertility such as:
  • Uterine Fibroids
  • PCOS
  • Thyroid Disorders
  • Frequent Miscarrage
  • Autoimmune Disorders
  • Endometriosis

6. Lifestyle Factors

Your lifestyle plays an important role in reproductive health. Bad lifestyle habits such as smoking, poor sleep quality, chronic stress, excessive alcohol consumption affects IVF success rate.

Frequently Asked Questions

What factors have the biggest impact on IVF success rates?

Several factors such as age of female, embryo quality, egg quality, sperm health and underlying medical conditions such as PCOS, miscarriage plays a crucial role in determining the IVF success rate.

How does age affect the chances of a successful IVF pregnancy?

Women under the age of 35 have the highest IVF success rates, with about 40% of IVF cycles resulting in a live birth. For women between the ages of 35 and 37, the success rate drops to about 30%, and for women over the age of 40, the success rate is around 15%.

Why do some IVF cycles fail despite good-quality embryos?

Even when high-quality embryos are transferred, successful implantation depends on multiple factors. Uterine receptivity, hormonal balance, immune-related issues, genetic abnormalities that may not be visible during embryo grading, and implantation-related challenges can all affect outcomes. In some cases, despite thorough evaluation and optimal treatment, implantation may not occur due to factors that are not fully understood by current medical science.

How do egg quality and sperm quality influence IVF outcomes?

The quality of the sperm and eggs is essential for producing healthy embryos. Successful fertilisation and embryo development are facilitated by healthy sperm with adequate motility, morphology, and DNA integrity. Reduced embryo quality and decreased odds of implantation and conception might result from low-quality eggs or sperm.

Does PCOS affect IVF success rates?

PCOS can cause ovulation problems and hormonal abnormalities in women. Patients with these diseases may benefit from early diagnosis, individualised treatment planning, and suitable fertility therapies.

Does the choice of IVF clinic affect treatment success rates?

Absolutely. IVF results can be greatly impacted by the experience of fertility professionals, laboratory standards, knowledge of embryology, and access to cutting-edge reproductive technologies. Is there any direct relationship between IVF Cost and Success Rate?

No, IVF success rates and cost are not directly correlated. There is no certainty that a more costly IVF procedure would increase the likelihood of becoming pregnant.
