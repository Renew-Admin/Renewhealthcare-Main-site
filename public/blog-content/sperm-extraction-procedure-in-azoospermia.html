Blocks in the epididymis and ejaculatory ducts may also cause obstructive azoospermia. The condition can be surgically cured. 

Non-Obstructive Azoospermia the body does not make any kind of sperm in this type of azoospermia. Or the sperm formation is too low and not visible in ejaculation.
