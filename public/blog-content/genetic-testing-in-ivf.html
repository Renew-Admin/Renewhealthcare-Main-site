Understanding the Role of Genetics in Fertility

Every couple’s fertility journey is unique. Sometimes, even after several attempts at conception, the reason for infertility remains hidden. That’s where genetics comes in. Advances in reproductive medicine now allow doctors to study how your genes influence egg and sperm quality, embryo development, and even implantation success. At the best IVF centre in Kolkata, experts use advanced genetic testing to identify hidden chromosomal or hereditary factors that could affect IVF success rates. This approach helps in creating a treatment plan that’s truly personalized.

Why Genetics Matter in IVF

Genetic testing during IVF treatment is not just about science:  it’s about giving hope to couples who have faced repeated failures or miscarriages. Understanding your genetic makeup can help: 
  1. Detect abnormalities in embryos before implantation
  2. Identify inherited disorders that may affect your baby’s health
  3. Improve the success rate of IVF treatment
  4. Reduce the chances of multiple failed cycles
This technology has completely changed how fertility specialists design treatment plans. Instead of trial and error, doctors now use precise, data-driven decisions for better outcomes.

The Benefits of Genetic Screening in IVF

At leading IVF hospitals in Jamshedpur, genetic screening has become a standard part of advanced IVF programs. Two key tests are often recommended: 
  • PGT-A (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Aneuploidies):  Helps identify embryos with the correct number of chromosomes, increasing the chance of a healthy pregnancy.
  • PGT-M (Preimplantation Genetic Testing for Monogenic Disorders):  Detects specific genetic diseases that might be passed down from parents to the child.
These tests ensure that only the healthiest embryos are transferred, which significantly improves the overall IVF success rate and reduces emotional and financial stress for couples.

Personalized IVF Treatment Plans

No two patients are the same:  and neither should their treatments be. Genetic insights allow fertility experts to create tailored plans based on each couple’s unique biology. For example, if testing shows certain genetic markers related to egg quality, your doctor might adjust stimulation medications or recommend additional support therapies. This kind of personalization can make a real difference in achieving a healthy pregnancy.

When to Consider Genetic Testing

Genetic testing is especially helpful if you: 
  • Have experienced two or more failed IVF cycles
  • Have a history of miscarriages
  • Are over 35 years old
  • Have a family history of inherited disorders
  • Want to ensure your baby’s long-term health
Even if you’re going through your first IVF cycle, discussing these options with your fertility expert can provide clarity and confidence in your treatment plan.
