Stress and Sperm Health: What is the Connection?

Psychological stress not only influences mental well-being or mood. It can also have a direct impact on the hormonal balance of the body. Healthy sperm production in men depends on a well-balanced system that is regulated by hormones like follicle-stimulating hormone (FSH), luteinizing hormone (LH), and testosterone. When the body is under chronic stress, it releases the stress hormone cortisol in larger amounts. The creation of testosterone is completed and its level is suppressed by this high cortisol production. As testosterone is critical for sperm development and libido, stress has a direct impact on sperm health. According to research and studies by an infertility specialist in Kolkata, men with high levels of psychological stress are often found to have lower sperm concentration, reduced sperm motility, and more abnormalities in sperm morphology. Such changes reduce the chances of successful fertilization significantly. Also, stress-induced oxidative stress can result in DNA fragmentation in sperm cells, which can reduce fertility even more. How Chronic Stress Disrupts Hormonal Balance?

The male reproductive system is under the direction of the hypothalamic-pituitary-gonadal (HPG) axis. This axis is actually a system of hormone production and feedback, which loops between the male brain and the testes. The axis gets disrupted when the body is under chronic stress. It can lead to imbalances in various reproductive hormones, such as testosterone. Stress can also affect various other systems in the male body that have an indirect impact on their fertility. It can reduce immune function, disrupt sleep, increase inflammation and promote unhealthy habits such as excessive drinking or smoking. All of these can ruin male reproductive health. Over a long period of time, it leads to a cycle in which emotional stress results in poor physical health and vice versa.
