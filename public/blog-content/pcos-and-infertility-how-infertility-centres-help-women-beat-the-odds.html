A complex hormonal disorder, polycystic ovarian Syndrome (PCOS) affects around 1 out of every 10 women of reproductive age across the world. This disorder is characterized by an imbalance in reproductive hormones, which results in various symptoms. Women with PCOS and infertility conditions have multiple small follicles on the ovaries, too many androgen levels, and irregular periods. Polycystic Ovarian Syndrome disrupts ovulation and interferes with fertility. When ovulation is not regular, there can be a significant decline in the cha33nces of natural conception. Excessive male hormones and higher insulin levels can reduce healthy eggs development and release.
