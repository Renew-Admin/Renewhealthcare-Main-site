Emotional Planning: How to Get Mentally Ready for IVF

It can be emotionally taxing to undergo IVF treatment. Both partners can find it stressful to have several medications, regular visits to the clinic and long waiting periods between sessions. However, accepting the emotional challenge associated with the process, right from the beginning, can help reduce mental pressure for couples. At most fertility centres, counselling has become more of a standard. It is now included in the treatment process. In 2025, fertility counsellors have a big role to play in preparing couples for the emotional demands associated with IVF. They help patients build mental strength and develop coping mechanisms to manage emotional hurdles during their IVF in Kolkata journey. Partners are also encouraged to maintain open communication with each other to strengthen mutual support—an essential part of handling the emotional highs and lows of in-vitro fertilization. Sharing experiences with IVF support groups, trusted family members, or close friends can offer relief and reduce the sense of isolation. Couples often find the journey more balanced and less stressful when they discuss possible outcomes, manage expectations, and set emotional boundaries in advance.
