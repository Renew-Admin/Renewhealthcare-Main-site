Consultation

Your journey will start with meeting your fertility consultant and having a proper consultation. During personal interaction, he can ask various things that you need to say clearly, so that he can have a better understanding of your case and your chances of fertility. Discuss any underlying issues that you may be having, and which might be contributing to delays in conception for you. You will probably be given a detailed health questionnaire, where you will be supposed to write about your menstrual cycles, infections and surgeries (if any), any history of genetic disorders, lifestyle habits, and so on. The health status and reproductive history of your male partner is also likely to be reviewed. During this stage, couples often inquire about the IVF treatment cost in Kolkata, as understanding the financial aspect is important for planning their fertility journey. Your consultant will review your medical records, discuss all your symptoms, and probably recommend some tests for you to undergo. This session can last anywhere from 45 to 60 minutes. You will get enough time to discuss your concerns and get some preliminary information about your chances of fertility, including factors that may influence the IVF success rate based on your individual case.  

Initial Tests and Evaluations

After your consultant completes the preliminary discussion, you will undergo a range of diagnostic tests. Women are likely to undergo blood tests for proper evaluation of their hormone levels, pelvic ultrasounds that can assess the status of their ovaries and uterus, and possibly HSG - which can evaluate their fallopian tube health. Men are likely to be recommended to undergo semen analysis for a proper assessment of their sperm count, morphology, and motility. With these initial tests, your doctor can create a baseline for your treatment plan. In the best clinics that offer IVF in Kolkata, you can expect ultrasound equipment and in-house labs. You can undergo many of these tests on the same day or schedule them at your convenience.  

Discussions about IVF Treatment Options and Costs

In the follow-up appointment, you will have to submit your test reports. Your doctor will review all these reports and outline various treatment options, based on your age, diagnosis, and reproductive goals. These could include in-vitro fertilization (IVF), intrauterine insemination (IUI), ovulation induction, or more state-of-the-art techniques such as ICSI (Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection). Your doctor will also discuss the IVF and IUI cost in Kolkata with you at this stage. Keep in mind that the cost can vary, based on how complex your case is, the number of cycles that you need, and whether you will require any cutting-edge techniques such as donor eggs/sperm, genetic testing or embryo freezing. The price range for a single IVF cycle can vary across clinics in Kolkata. However, most of the reputed facilities are fully transparent about the costs and you can plan better with package options and itemized billing.  

Follow-Up and Treatment

When you are okay with a specific treatment plan, you will probably be assigned a nurse coordinator or case manager, who will guide you through treatment timelines, give you instructions about taking medications, and schedule your following appointments. You can expect clear and timely communication at the best IVF facilities in Kolkata. Clinics that are known for high IVF success rates can deliver online portals, mobile apps and 24/7 helplines for your support and convenience. With the progress of treatment, you can expect regular check-ins for hormone level evaluation, ultrasound monitoring and embryo development updates (if you choose IVF treatment). With all these follow-ups, you can have optimal chances of success at each phase of treatment.
