Which Fertility-Boosting Foods Should Women Have?

Women need to have nutrient-rich foods that can support their reproductive function and hormonal balance. Swiss chard, kale, spinach, and other leafy green vegetables can be essential for their body. These are rich in the B-vitamin known as folate, which is essential for proper foetal development and healthy ovulation. These also have antioxidants that can protect reproductive cells from damage. They can get complex carbohydrates from oats, brown rice and other whole grains that support hormonal balance and maintain stable blood sugar levels in the body. Insulin resistance and high blood sugar can affect ovulation negatively. So, it is better for women to have whole grains instead of refined carbs to get effective results. What are the Essential Supplements for Female Fertility?

The female body can get most of the necessary nutrients from a well-balanced diet. However, the intake of certain supplements can support their fertility further, especially when there are specific deficiencies or when their dietary intake is insufficient. Women can do better with folic acid, possibly the most well-known fertility supplement. It can support egg health and also prevent neural tube defects during pregnancy. Females who want to conceive need to have a minimum of 400 micrograms folic acid every day, even before they get pregnant.
