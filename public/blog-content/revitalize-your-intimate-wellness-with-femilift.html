What is Femilift?

Femilift is a non-surgical vaginal rejuvenation treatment that uses a laser to stimulate collagen production and improve vaginal health. The laser technology used in Femilift is a fractional CO2 laser, which delivers controlled energy to the vaginal tissue. This stimulates the production of collagen, the protein responsible for the strength and elasticity of the skin and connective tissue.

What issues does Femilift solve?

Femilift is a non-surgical vaginal rejuvenation treatment that can help address several vaginal health concerns, including: Vaginal dryness: Vaginal dryness is a common problem due to hormonal changes, menopause, breastfeeding, or certain medications. Femilift can help improve vaginal moisture by stimulating collagen production in the vaginal tissue. Vaginal laxity: Vaginal laxity is a condition where the vaginal walls lose their elasticity, leading to a feeling of looseness or sagging. This can occur due to childbirth, ageing, or hormonal changes. Femilift can help tighten and tone the vaginal walls by stimulating collagen production. Urinary incontinence: Urinary incontinence is a condition where there is the involuntary leakage of urine due to weak pelvic floor muscles. Femilift can help strengthen the pelvic floor muscles by stimulating collagen production and reducing urinary incontinence symptoms. Pain during intercourse: Pain during intercourse can occur due to vaginal dryness, vaginal laxity, or other vaginal health concerns. Femilift can improve vaginal moisture and tighten the vaginal walls, reducing pain and discomfort during intercourse. Vaginal rejuvenation: Vaginal rejuvenation refers to improving the overall health and appearance of the vagina. Femilift can help improve vaginal moisture, tone and tighten the vaginal walls, and reduce urinary incontinence symptoms, leading to overall vaginal rejuvenation.

How does Femilift work?

During the Femilift treatment, the laser is applied to the vaginal wall, where it creates tiny columns of thermal injury, known as microthermal zones. These zones trigger the body's natural healing response, leading to the production of new collagen fibres. As collagen fibres form, they improve the strength and elasticity of the vaginal tissue, leading to tighter and more toned vaginal walls. Additionally, increased collagen production can improve vaginal moisture, reducing dryness and discomfort during intercourse.

Are there any side effects of Femilift?

Femilift is a safe and well-tolerated treatment, and there are typically no significant side effects associated with the procedure. Some patients may experience mild discomfort or spotting immediately following the treatment, but these symptoms usually resolve quickly.

How do I know if FemiLift is right for me?

If you are experiencing vaginal health concerns such as vaginal dryness, laxity, or urinary incontinence, Femilift may be a suitable treatment for you.
