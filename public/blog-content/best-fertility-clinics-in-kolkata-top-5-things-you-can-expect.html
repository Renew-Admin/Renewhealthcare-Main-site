2. What is the success rate of IVF treatment? 

Success rates for IVF vary from couple to couple. Age and quality of eggs and sperm and the cause of the infertility are the most important factors. Younger patients usually have better odds. A good clinic will tell you straight up about your chances with appropriate testing and not guarantee you a successful outcome. 

3. When should a couple see a fertility specialist? 

Most doctors recommend consulting a fertility specialist if you have been trying to conceive for one year without success or six months if the woman is over 35. If you have known issues, such as PCOS, irregular periods or previous miscarriages, it's worth getting checked out sooner rather than later.
