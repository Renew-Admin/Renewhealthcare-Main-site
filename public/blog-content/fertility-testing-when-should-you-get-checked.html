What is Fertility Testing? 

  An important diagnostic process, Fertility Testing is used for proper identification of what are causing infertility and why conception has become difficult.   The process is conducted for both men and women, and involves tests that can help to know which underlying issues are leading to infertility, so that proper diagnosis and treatment are possible.   With results for many fertility tests available in just a few days, the procedure can be completed rather quickly.   

When Should You Go for Fertility Testing? 

  Typically, it is recommended if you are less than 35 years of age and have been making repeated unsuccessful attempts to conceive for a year. It is also a good time to consider this testing if you are aged 35 years or higher, and have been making unsuccessful conception attempts for at least 6 months.   With advancing age, fertility decreases. This is true for both senses. However, female fertility declines naturally with age and earlier than that of males. After age 35, there is a significant drop in the fertility of women. Men generally begin to witness a reduction in fertility capabilities after 40 years of age. If you are older than 35 (and happen to be a woman) or 40 (happening to be a man), it is advisable that you seek early assessment if you are facing conception issues.   If you have some medical concerns or have a medical history of some health problems already, you might like to get in touch with a doctor sooner, and have a proper consultation. Some health issues may affect fertility. If you or your partner suffer from irregular periods, pelvic inflammatory disease, have a history of STIs or suffer from any related issue, consult a doctor sooner.   As both men and women can suffer from fertility problems, it is important for both partners to get evaluated.    

How Fertility Testing Helps? 

  Fertility tests are essential for couples who want to know about the exact causes of their infertility. These may include structural issues in the reproductive organs, hormonal imbalances, poor sperm quality or ovulation problems.   With fertility test reports, doctors can come up with a suitable treatment plan. This might include medication, assisted reproductive technologies (ART) like IVF from a top IVF center in Kolkata or surgery.   Hormone Blood Tests, Ultrasound, Hysterosalpingogram (HSG), Laparoscopy, Ovarian Reserve Tests and Ovulation Tests are some of the common fertility tests done in women.   Semen Analysis, Hormone Blood Tests and Testicular Biopsy if required are some of the common fertility tests for men.   If you are over 35 or 40 years of age, and finding it difficult to achieve successful fertility, go for Fertility Testing at the best IVF center in Kolkata and get yourself checked.

FAQ – Frequently Asked Questions

 

  • Which is the most important fertility test? 
Hysteroscopy is arguably the most important one, as it helps investigate problems related to heavy and irregular bleeding, recurrent miscarriage or infertility. It can also help to look for issue such as scarring within the uterine lining, polyps and fibroids etc.   
  • Which tests do women undergo for fertility testing? 
Initial blood tests like TSH, PRL, AMH, HPLC & Rubella, IgA need to be done. Blood can also be tested for the LH, to check for ovulation. With women having irregular periods, blood tests can be done at IVF center for an assessment of the hormones known as gonadotropins which help in egg production.    
  • Which one is the most common fertility testing for men? 
A semen analysis is the most common fertility test for males. It helps to analyze semen sample to determine sperm count, shape and movement. These are the most important indicators of fertility in case of male partner.
