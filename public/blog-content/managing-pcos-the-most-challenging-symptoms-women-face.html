Are you, or any other woman close to you, suffering from Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS)? For the uninitiated, this is a common hormonal disorder that affects females of reproductive age. This is a gynecological issue and this hormonal condition affects the reproductive system and ovaries specifically. The ovaries are responsible for the production of hormones and eggs, and these functions are disrupted due to PCOS. Take a look at the most challenging symptoms that women with PCOS face.

  1. Menstrual Irregularities

These are one of the commonest issues faced by women with PCOS. Many women having PCOS tend to have no period or even when they have periods, they are infrequent and can be prolonged or marked by heavy bleeding. This can be a major cause for concern in women with PCOS. When periods are absent or irregular, they can raise the risk of endometrial overgrowth (hyperplasia). In some cases, even endometrial cancer cannot be ruled out.

  1. Infertility

The inability or difficulty to reproduce is obviously, one of the marked symptoms of PCOS. Due to lack of ovulation or irregular ovulation, women with Polycystic ovary syndrome can find it tough to get pregnant.

  1. Excess Hair Growth (Hirsutism)

It is another major issue in many women with PCOS, who tend to suffer from the growth of too much hair in all those areas where men generally have hair, such as the abdomen, back, chest and face. While unwanted hair does nor pose any physical problems, it can be a cause for embarrassment and dampen the confidence of women who suffer from the condition. They have to devote a lot of time to shave the facial area and shave or hide the rest as much as possible, while going out in public.

  1. Acne

Acne problems can also be common in PCOS sufferers. Hormonal imbalances related to the condition can give rise to acne, particularly on the face, back and chest area.

  1. Weight Gain and Obesity

Weight problems are also noticed by many women affected with PCOS. They struggle with keeping their weight under control. It can be challenging for them to lose weight, especially from around the belly region.

  1. Other Potential Health Issues

PCOS can also give rise to various other health issues. Women with this gynecological problem can have greater risk of heart disease, high blood pressure and type 2 diabetes. They are at a higher risk of developing such conditions. It has also been noted that women with PCOS suffer from depression, anxiety and mood swings. The challenges of living with PCOS and the associated hormonal changes can be quite tough for sufferers. As evident, Polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) can lead women to face many challenging issues. It is necessary for sufferers to approach a reputed fertility clinic, where an expert gynecologist or reproductive endocrinologist can diagnose and manage PCOS properly. With the aid of medications to regulate hormones, lifestyle changes and even fertility treatments in some cases, they can help women to restore their health.
