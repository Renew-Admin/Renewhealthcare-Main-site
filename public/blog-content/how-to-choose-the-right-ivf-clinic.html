The Types and Variety of IVF Treatments Offered

 

To understand IVF means, you need to understand the types and varieties of IVF treatment.  Infertility treatment nowadays is so far from merely the process of traditional IVF. A variety of infertility and genetic factors in the male and/or the female may require other additional treatments and services in order to provide the best possible outcomes. 

Where your infertility issues are not severe, the doctors may advise you to try IUI first before proceeding with IVF. You may need ICSI instead of the traditional IVF for your fertilization, or the male may require PESA/TESE to extract sperm, or Sperm MACS or PICSI to select the few good sperm. During the IVF cycle, you may also need a clinic which provides egg freezing to save your retrieved eggs for later use. 

After the IVF cycle, you will most likely to need a clinic which provides embryo freezing to preserve your good embryos for later use. A good IVF clinic in Kolkata may also provide PGT services, genetic testing of embryos prior to transfer to the woman’s uterus, where patients require genetic screening of their embryos for a variety of reasons. Always look for the followings:

  • Does the clinic have an in-house Geneticist?
  • Does the clinic have a qualified andrologist (male fertility specialist)?
  • Does the clinic have a full time qualified embryologist?

To be double sure, request for a visit to the IVF lab to see what kind of technology they have, and understand the IVF process step by step. How well are they equipped & how experienced & confident are the embryology team. 

It boils down to basically that the clinic's range of assisted reproductive services indicate how likely it will be that the clinic can support your needs through every step of your fertility treatment journey.

IVF Treatment Cost and Affordability

   

Although most couples seeking IVF treatment are already aware that IVF is generally quite costly, it is still always wise to spend some time researching costs and searching for a clinic with a reputation of no hidden costs, that is open about the range of expenses, that will take time to explain the potential costs and the reasons why certain treatments may be suggested or recommended. Packages can be confusing and misleading. 

To find the right IVF clinic among multiple IVF hospital in Kolkata, you should ask following questions during cost counselling, please note 

  • Whether the clinic takes time to explain both what is included in a package?
  • What is not included? 
  • What are the procedures that you need as a patient?
  • Why do you need them? 
  • What is the cost of consecutive cycles in case the first IVF fails?

IVF Specialist: Qualifications and Experiences of Doctors

The qualifications and experience of the doctors at each IVF clinic will likely be a major part of your decision-making. Do the IVF specialists have the highest training possible in infertility and reproduction? Though it seems to have a fixed set of steps, IVF treatment can require very subtle changes between patients to achieve the best result. 

A good IVF clinic should design a personalized treatment plan for each patient. Each patients’ response to medications is different, and the process of stimulation and egg retrieval needs to be closely monitored to avoid any complications. Making an appointment and talking to the doctor in person may give you a better understanding of whether it is the right IVF clinic for you. If he or she is a good infertility clinician, it will usually not take long to be obvious.

Quality of the Staff
