What are the Signs of Good and Bad Menstrual Health? 

  These are some of the signs of good menstrual health:   
  • Regular Cycle:  Typically, doctors regard a cycle duration of 21-35 days as normal. 
  • Blood Colour:  Healthy menstrual blood can be bright to dark red or even brown in color, based on the flow stage.  
  • Consistency in Flow:  Generally, periods last anywhere between 3 and 7 days. However, there are variations in blood flow volume.  
  • Mild to Moderate Cramping:  It is common to suffer from cramps or pain in the abdomen, but these should not be debilitating or acute.  
  • Other Normal Symptoms:  Fatigue, mood swings, breast tenderness and bloating are some other symptoms that menstruating women often report of.  
  Poor menstrual health is manifested by these symptoms: 
  • Heavy Bleeding:  If women bleed for over 7 days, suffer from large blood clots, or need to change their tampon or pad often, they are unlikely to be in good menstrual health.  
  • Missing Periods:  Those who miss 3 periods or more in a row, especially when not pregnant, may also need to see a doctor. 
  • Severe Pain: Poor menstrual health is also reflected by acute cramping during or between periods. Women can also feel debilitating pain that affects their daily activities. 
  • Irregular Cycles:  If the periods are too long, too short, or come and go unpredictably, the menstrual health might not be in good shape.   
  • Bleeding Between Periods:  Bleeding or spotting between regular menstrual periods can also be the reason for worry. 
  • Other Symptoms:  Fever, fatigue, foul-smelling vaginal discharge, shortness of breath, or low energy after using tampons can also be the symptoms of poor menstrual health.
