Medical Fertility Treatments: From Medications to IVF 

  Often, medical infertility treatment in Kolkata is the first step for women to address infertility issues. Doctors may prescribe Letrozole, Clomiphene Citrate or other medications to help stimulate their ovulation. With such kinds of drugs, their egg release can be induced or regulated. This is particularly true for women having irregular menstrual cycles.  In case doctors find that medications are not working, they might recommend women to go for IUI. In this method, sperm is directly inserted into the uterus of women at the time of ovulation. It increases fertilization chances. Often, IUI is used along with fertility drugs to improve IVF success rates.  In vitro fertilization (IVF) is among the treatment approaches known most and used most widely. Eggs are extracted from the ovaries, fertilized with sperm in a lab, and the resulting embryos are then returned to the uterus during IVF in Kolkata. Often, this method is recommended for females with severe endometriosis, blocked fallopian tubes, cases of unexplained infertility, even when 3 or more IUI cycles fail.  Doctors can also use IVF along with other methods such as preimplantation genetic testing (PGT) or Intracytoplasmic Sperm Injection (ICSI), to improve outcomes.   For women who want to delay their pregnancy, doctors may go for medical interventions like egg or embryo freezing. When it is medically not viable for women to carry a pregnancy, surrogacy or donor eggs may also be recommended.   

Natural and Holistic Approaches to Boost Fertility 

  While medical treatments can yield good results, many women like to go for complementary remedies or natural remedies alongside medical procedures or even before they seek clinical assistance. Such kinds of approaches focus on overall health improvement and try to address all those causes of infertility that arise due to poor lifestyle habits.  With proper nutrition and diet, it can be easier to improve overall reproductive health. Women should consume a balanced diet that is rich in minerals, vitamins, healthy fats, and antioxidants. These can support the hormonal balance in their body and improve their quality of eggs. They should also avoid consuming excessive caffeine, alcohol, and processed foods. Based on naturopathic and scientific guidelines, some women are recommended to go for specific fertility diets.   A sufficient amount of sleep, proper stress management techniques, and regular exercise can also have a direct, positive impact on fertility. Chronic stress can be disruptive to hormonal balance and ovulation. Women are recommended to go for mind-body practices such as acupuncture, meditation, and yoga, which can reduce their stress levels and improve their reproductive wellness. These can show especially promising results in controlling their menstrual cycles and improving the flow of blood to their reproductive organs.   Herbal remedies, such as evening primrose oil, Vitex (chaste tree berry), maca root and other supplements are often used by many women who want to improve their fertility naturally. However, before they start taking any supplement, they need to consult a qualified healthcare provider. This is because some herbs may interfere with their underlying conditions or with any medication that they may be taking for any health issue.  Women should also try to address vitamin deficiencies, insulin resistance, thyroid dysfunction or any other underlying medical issue through natural treatment methods. Such methods can help to improve their chances of fertility with no need to go for more invasive interventions.
